## Real data diagnostics

Dataset `ds_6f37a2401444b6ea` contains stored IEX minute bars for AAPL, MSFT, QQQ and SPY. Every model within a
tournament used identical deterministic training/calibration/test rows from four purged development folds.
Complete minute-grid target windows were required. Row sampling was fixed before forecasts were inspected.

The supervised tournament used 256 training rows and 32 OOS decisions per fold: **128 OOS decisions per model**.
All 13 candidates completed. LightGBM, DoubleEnsemble and XGBoost took respectively 2, 3 and 4 trades with positive
net results. Other candidates either abstained or lost money on their few trades. These are very small samples;
**every paired incremental-value test failed credibility**. No winner was promoted.

The foundation tournament used 64 training rows and 8 OOS decisions per fold: **32 OOS decisions per model**.
All requested pretrained challengers completed local inference and paired diagnostics. FinText's initial large
sampling batch was interrupted and recorded as failed; an eight-series-batched rerun completed on the same
predeclared rows alongside Ridge. Failed trials remain recorded. Every paired test again failed credibility.
FinCast, Simons and Mitra selected the same single profitable trade as Ridge; this establishes no incremental value.

The earlier Kronos-base tournament and actual LEAN replay are documented separately in
[kronos-integration-2026-11-18.md](kronos-integration-2026-11-18.md). That replay produced no orders because the
model produced no positive cost/uncertainty-adjusted signals. The positive-score LEAN fixture tested plumbing only.

Volatility results on the 128 supervised-tournament OOS rows:

| Model | QLIKE (lower is better) | Volatility RMSE, bps |
|---|---:|---:|
| HAR-RV | 3.9510 | 22.29 |
| EWMA | 0.2232 | 15.85 |
| GJR-GARCH | 0.2470 | 12.41 |

GJR improves RMSE while EWMA improves QLIKE. This is mixed development evidence, not a demonstrated trading benefit.
The HMM and Markov-switching outputs are filtered regime features, not externally validated regime ground truth.

## Boundaries that remain

This is not evidence of sustainable positive ROI and is not a completed full-budget independent selection campaign.
Foundation challengers remain **research-only**: pretraining overlap and prospective sequence confirmation/runtime
promotion are unresolved. The independently validated paper route supports the trainable local models and ensembles.

The application currently uses `ai_transport=fake`. Live RD-Agent LLM proposals were not enabled or tested with paid
credentials. The implemented `--rd-agent` path requires the existing real-provider configuration. The upstream
unrestricted RD-Agent generated-code loop is deliberately replaced by the application's controlled experiment workers.

Kronos bounded head fine-tuning, local supervised training, masked pretraining and meta-initialization are implemented.
The upstream full-model/CUDA fine-tuning recipes for every pretrained foundation model have **not** all been ported.
Contextual bandit/offline RL mechanics were tested using synthetic logged-data fixtures; meaningful policy improvement
requires actual behavior logs with recorded propensities. No synthetic policy result authorizes deployment.
